GPUMD-PySAGES: GPU-Native Enhanced Sampling on Machine-Learning Potentials

Motivation Molecular dynamics (MD) simulations with machine-learned interatomic potentials — particularly the neuroevolution potential (NEP) framework in GPUMD — offer an excellent balance of accuracy and speed on GPUs. However, GPUMD has historically lacked native support for enhanced-sampling methods like metadynamics, umbrella sampling, or ABF. PySAGES , on the other hand, provides a JAX-based suite of enhanced-sampling methods with automatic differentiation and GPU-accelerated collective variable (CV) computations.
